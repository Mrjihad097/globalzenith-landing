05 / WHITEPAPER

Zenith documentation.

The core project information, token utility, presale framework, liquidity strategy, vesting, governance, transparency and risk disclosure are summarized below.

1. Introduction

Zenith (ZENI) is a BEP-20 utility token designed to build a community-driven ecosystem focused on user engagement, participation, rewards, and future community governance. Zenith aims to give users opportunities to participate in campaigns, complete tasks, earn rewards, and contribute opinions on selected future ecosystem decisions. ZENI is intended primarily as a utility and community participation token, rather than a promise of profit or a guaranteed investment return.

2. Vision

Zenith's vision is to create an active ecosystem where users can participate in campaigns and community activities while having a meaningful role in the future development of the project.

  • Community participation
  • Campaign-based rewards
  • Airdrop programs
  • User engagement
  • Transparent token distribution
  • Community feedback and voting

4. Presale

Zenith plans a presale containing 200,000,000 ZENI (20% of total supply). Participants may contribute supported assets such as BNB or USDT according to the final presale smart-contract rules. The final ZENI price, presale duration, minimum/maximum contribution, hard cap, accepted assets and claim schedule will be publicly announced before the presale begins.

5. Security & Transparency

Zenith intends to prioritize transparency. The project will provide publicly verifiable information wherever possible, including the token contract, tokenomics, allocation information, vesting schedules, liquidity information, official wallet addresses and major ecosystem updates.

6. Liquidity Strategy

Following the presale, Zenith plans to establish liquidity on a suitable decentralized exchange on BNB Smart Chain. An initial target allocation is 100,000,000 ZENI (10% of total supply). The exact amount and pairing, such as ZENI/BNB or ZENI/USDT, will depend on the final launch strategy. The project intends to lock initial liquidity for a publicly announced period using an appropriate liquidity-lock mechanism.

7. Vesting & Token Unlock

To reduce sudden selling pressure, Zenith can use a structured vesting system. A proposed presale structure is 25% available at TGE and 75% released gradually over the following 6 months. Development, marketing and ecosystem allocations may also be released gradually according to predefined schedules. If a team allocation is introduced in the future, a longer vesting period with a cliff may be used.

8. Utility

ZENI is designed to have utility within the future Zenith ecosystem. Potential utility includes campaign rewards for eligible activities, community participation, educational activities, special events and airdrops. The airdrop allocation is 50,000,000 ZENI (5%) and may be distributed through multiple campaigns.

9. Community Governance

ZENI holders may be given the opportunity to participate in community voting on selected future ecosystem decisions. Potential topics include campaign ideas, community initiatives, ecosystem priorities, reward-program proposals and selected development proposals. Holding ZENI does not automatically mean that every project decision is legally or technically controlled by token holders. The project can initially use community proposals and off-chain voting, and potentially introduce a dedicated governance system later.

10. Community & Ecosystem

The long-term goal is to create an ecosystem where community members are active participants. Future initiatives may follow: Campaign → Participation → Rewards → Community Feedback → Ecosystem Growth.

11. Transparency Commitments
  • Token contract and verification
  • Total supply and allocations
  • Presale rules and vesting schedules
  • Liquidity and liquidity-lock information
  • Official wallet addresses
  • Airdrop criteria
  • Major ecosystem updates
12. Risk Disclosure

ZENI is a digital token and its market value may fluctuate significantly. Purchasing ZENI carries risks including market volatility, loss of value, liquidity risk, smart-contract risk, regulatory uncertainty, technical risks and DEX-related risks. Nothing in this document should be interpreted as a guarantee of profit, future token price or investment return. Users should conduct their own research and understand the risks before participating.

13. Conclusion

Zenith (ZENI) aims to build a transparent, community-focused utility ecosystem on BNB Smart Chain with a fixed total supply of 1 billion ZENI, structured community participation, liquidity strategy, vesting mechanisms, rewards, airdrop campaigns and potential future governance.
